# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community. Everyone
who participates in this project should be treated with dignity and respect,
regardless of identity, background, experience, ability, or point of view.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and assume good intent while still addressing
  harmful outcomes;
- give specific, constructive technical feedback;
- accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes;
- respect privacy, personal boundaries, and differing levels of experience;
- focus disagreement on ideas and project outcomes rather than people;
- help maintain an environment where others can participate safely.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption;
- discriminatory, sexualized, insulting, or demeaning language or imagery;
- personal attacks or deliberate humiliation;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- pressuring someone to disclose personal information;
- retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith;
- conduct that a reasonable person would consider unsafe or inappropriate in
  a professional open-source community.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone officially
represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report possible violations privately to
[s.emad.helmi@gmail.com](mailto:s.emad.helmi@gmail.com). Include relevant links,
context, and any supporting material that can be shared safely.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Information
may be shared only when needed to investigate, protect participants, or meet a
legal obligation. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is prohibited.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this policy. A
response will be proportionate to the behavior, its impact, prior incidents,
and the safety of the community. Possible actions include:

1. a private correction and request to change behavior;
2. a formal warning and conditions for continued participation;
3. temporary restriction from project interaction;
4. permanent removal from project spaces.

The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation immediately when
that is necessary to protect people or the project.
